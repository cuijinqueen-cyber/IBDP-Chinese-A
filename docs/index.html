Five recurring patterns（背熟）

  1. Reasons + Positive/Negative
  2. Reasons + Should
  3. Problems/Causes + Solutions
  4. Factors + Evaluation (How realistic?)
  5. Reasons + Effects

题型识别关键词

  • Why…? / What problems…? / In what ways…? / What factors…?
  • Is this positive or negative? / Should…? / What can be done…? / How realistic…?
  • 必须 两个问题都充分回答

1.1 审题 90 秒标准动作

Step问自己产出
1背景话题？引言改写
2Q1 类型？reasons / problems / effects / ways / factors
3Q2 类型？solutions / pos-neg / should / realistic / effects
4是否双对象？individuals and society 等
5两问是否同类？不同类则不混段
6结论收什么？Q1 一句 + Q2 一句

真题 A 深拆： Q1 problems（个人+社会）→ Q2 solutions（个人+政府）。忌只写养老金或空泛 “government should help”。

真题 B 深拆： Q1=关系形态变化；Q2=必须总体积极/消极。忌骑墙。

真题 C 深拆： Q1=产业原因；Q2=条件性 should。忌文化抒情无立场。

1.2 Band 7 计划板（可投影）

Ageing： BP1 医疗/养老压力；BP2 健康习惯 + 社区养老/养老金改革。

Technology： BP1 远距离关系/弱连接/面对面减少；BP2 明确总体评价。

Films： BP1 预算营销语言；BP2 有限资助保文化、看质量。

1.3 6.5 vs 7.0 对照

点6.57.0–7.5
覆盖第二问偏短两问充分
分段混写一问一段
第二问任务写错严格匹配
评价题有好有坏有总体立场
例子空泛有机制短例

2. 为什么 6.5 学生在 Two-part 容易卡住

问题6.5 常见表现Band 7 目标
只答一问原因写很长，第二问草草两句两问篇幅大致均衡（约 50/50）
两问混在一段读起来乱，中心不清一问一段（或清晰的两段分工）
第二问跑题题目问 positive/negative，却只写解决方案严格按第二问任务写
例子空泛Technology is bad for relationships具体机制 + 例子
立场不清It has both sides 无决断评价类第二问要有清晰倾向

教学优先级

  1. 审题：拆出 Q1 / Q2 任务类型（TR）
  2. 均衡覆盖与段落分工（CC / TR）
  3. 因果 / 评价复杂句准确（GRA）
  4. 话题词汇自然升级（LR）

3. Band 7 推荐结构

结构 A（最稳，先教）

段落任务
Introduction改写话题 + 用一句话预告将回答两个问题
Body 1完整回答 Question 1
Body 2完整回答 Question 2
Conclusion各用一句收束两个答案（不引入新点）

结构 B（第二问是 opinion 时）

  • BP1：Reasons / Effects（Q1）
  • BP2：Clear opinion + support（Q2）
  • 结论：重申评价立场

时间

  • 计划 5 分钟（必须写出 Q1/Q2 关键词）
  • 写作 32–33 分钟
  • 检查 2–3 分钟（专查：两问是否都答了）

5. 详细教案

Lesson 1 — 诊断与目标设定（90 min）

真题 A

In many countries, people are living longer than ever before. What problems does this cause for individuals and society? What can individuals and governments do to deal with these problems?

流程

  1. 热身（10 min）

问：这篇要回答几个问题？两问一样重要吗？

  1. 限时写作（40 min）
  2. 自查（15 min）
  • Q1 有独立充分段落
  • Q2 有独立充分段落
  • 结论覆盖两问
  • 无跑题
  1. 口头反馈（25 min） — Top 3 only

作业

  • 只重写 introduction（明确预告两问）。
  • 建 Error Log。

Lesson 2 — 架构与审题拆分（90 min）

目标： 装上 “Q1 一段 + Q2 一段” 系统。

板书拆题公式

Step 1: 话题是什么？

Step 2: Question 1 任务类型？

Step 3: Question 2 任务类型？

Step 4: 每问准备 2 个可展开点

真题 A 示范计划

问题任务内容点
Q1Problems for individuals & society个人：医疗开支/生活质量；社会：养老金与医疗系统压力
Q2What individuals & governments can do个人：健康生活方式；政府：养老金改革、社区养老与医疗投入

流程

  1. 拆题训练（20 min）— 3 道 two-part 只拆不写
  2. 结构建模（20 min）
  3. “偏科文”对比（15 min）：Q2 只有两句的 6.5 范文 vs 均衡 7.0
  4. 写 Body 1（Q1）限时（25 min）
  5. 收束（10 min）

课堂练习： A1 题型识别 · A2 均衡检查 · A3 连接词 · A4 写 Q1 段

加练（10 min）： 从分析库抽 Exam 2 / 7 / 13，只做 Q1/Q2 类型标注 + 主题句。

作业

  • 真题 A 全文（强调 Q2 不少于 Q1 的 80% 字数）。
  • A5 第二问任务匹配。
  • 阅读 ielts-two-part-past-exams-analysis.md 中 Exam 2 与 Exam 11 的 Trap analysis。

Lesson 3 — Q1 深挖（90 min）

目标： 把 “problems / reasons / effects” 写透，不列点。

发展链

Point → Why / How → Example → Result for individuals or society

升级示例

  • 6.5：Old people need more money and hospitals are busy.
  • 7.0：Because longer lifespans increase demand for long-term healthcare, public hospitals face heavier caseloads; at the same time, pension systems struggle when fewer workers support more retirees.

流程

  1. 作业反馈（20 min）— 先看两问是否都出现
  2. Q1 机制写作坊（30 min）
  3. 限时重写 BP1（25 min）
  4. 选点训练：删掉最弱的第三点（15 min）

练习： B1 搭配 · B2 词汇升级 · B3 词形

作业

  • 定稿 Q1 段 + 自查表。
  • B4 短语造句。

Lesson 4 — Q2 深挖（solutions / opinion）（90 min）

目标： 第二问写满，且任务正确。

若 Q2 = solutions

  • Who + How + Expected result
  • 尽量对应 Q1 的问题（matching）

若 Q2 = positive/negative 或 should…

  • 明确立场（总体积极 / 总体消极 / 有条件支持）
  • 2 个支持理由 + 1 个具体例子
  • 可用一句让步，但不能变成无立场

真题 A 对应表

Q1 problemQ2 response
Healthcare pressureGovernments expand community care / preventive health
Pension burdenGradual retirement age reform + sustainable funding
Individual loneliness / costsIndividuals plan savings; stay active; family + community support

流程

  1. 空泛方案清理（15 min）
  2. 对应表填空（20 min）
  3. 限时写 BP2（30 min）
  4. 结论两句操练（20 min）
  5. 总结（5 min）

练习： B5 完型 · D1 搭配 · D2 复合句

作业

  • 真题 A 二稿（两问字数差控制在 20% 内）。
  • D3 改错。

Lesson 5 — Lexis & Grammar Lift（90 min）

高频雷区

  • 漏答第二问标记词（should / positive / problems）
  • a solution of → a solution to
  • the reason of → the reason for / why
  • affect / effect 混用
  • although… but

高价值句型

  1. One major problem is that…
  2. This can be addressed by…
  3. In terms of relationships, technology has…
  4. Overall, I would argue that this is largely a positive/negative development because…

流程

  1. Error Log（20 min）
  2. 双问衔接句（20 min）
  3. 话题词库：老龄化 / 科技社交（25 min）
  4. 最弱段重写（25 min）

练习： D4–D6 · E1 段落升级

作业

  • 句型转换 10 题。
  • 词库各 8 句。

Lesson 6 — 迁移真题 B（90 min）

真题 B

Nowadays the way many people interact with each other has changed because of technology. In what ways has technology affected the types of relationships people make? Has this become a positive or negative development?

审题重点

  • Q1：in what ways = 影响/变化方式（不是简单利弊清单）
  • Q2：positive or negative = 必须表态

推荐计划

  • BP1：线上弱连接变多；跨距离关系更易维持；部分面对面深度下降
  • BP2：总体 mixed but largely positive / largely negative（选一侧并支持）

流程

  1. 5 分钟拆题比赛（10 min）
  2. 计划板（20 min）
  3. 限时双主体段（40 min）
  4. 与真题 A 迁移复盘（20 min）

练习： C1–C4 科技/人际关系词汇

作业

  • 真题 B 全文。
  • 用荧光笔标出 Q1 句与 Q2 句。

Lesson 7 — 限时真题 C（90 min）

真题 C

Many people prefer to watch foreign films rather than locally produced films. Why is this? Should governments give financial support to local film industries?

计划示范

  • Q1 Why：制作预算与特效；全球明星与营销；英语片国际传播
  • Q2 Should：有条件支持 — 保护文化多样性，但应透明、鼓励质量而非保护落后

流程

  1. 策略复述（10 min）
  2. 全程限时（40 min）
  3. 与 Lesson 1 对比（25 min）
  4. E3 综合小测（15 min）

作业

  • 重写更弱的一问。
  • 继续 Section F。

Lesson 8 — 终测与反馈（90 min）

  1. 新 two-part 模考（40–45 min）
  2. 四项评分会议（35 min）
  3. 考前维护（10 min）：每周 3 篇 two-part；每天检查 “两问是否均衡”

2. Full Analyses

Exam 1 — Happiness

Prompt

Happiness is considered very important in life. Why is it difficult to define? What factors are important in achieving happiness?

Task split

  • Q1: reasons (definition difficulty)
  • Q2: factors (not solutions; not advantages)

Trap analysis

  • Students write “how to be happy” as tips only, ignoring why definition is hard.
  • Q2 becomes a lifestyle advice list with no ranking.
  • Philosophical over-writing wastes time.

Band 7 plan

ParaContent
IntroHappiness is widely valued, but hard to define; this essay explains why and key factors
BP1 Q1Subjective meaning; culture/age differences; changes across life stages
BP2 Q2Health + relationships + purposeful work/security (choose 2–3, develop)
ConcHard to define because personal/cultural; main factors: …

Lexis: subjective, wellbeing, life stage, sense of purpose, financial security, interpersonal relationships

Grammar: One reason why… is that… / What matters most is…

6.5 → 7 upgrade

  • 6.5: Happiness means different things.
  • 7.0: Happiness is difficult to define because it is highly subjective: for some it means emotional calm, while for others it means achievement or social status.

Teaching note: Force students to answer Q1 with definition difficulty, not “people are unhappy”.

Exam 2 — Job satisfaction (Cambridge IELTS 7)

Prompt

As most people spend a major part of their adult life at work, job satisfaction is an important element of individual wellbeing. What factors contribute to job satisfaction? How realistic is the expectation of job satisfaction for all workers?

Task split

  • Q1: factors
  • Q2: evaluation / realistic? (opinion-like judgement)

Trap analysis

  • Q2 ignored or answered with “yes everyone can be happy at work”.
  • Q1 lists 6 factors with no depth.
  • No link between Q1 factors and Q2 realism.

Band 7 plan

ParaContent
BP1Meaningful work, fair pay, relationships/management, growth chances
BP2Not fully realistic for all: labour market limits, routine jobs, economic pressure; but can be improved for many
ConcKey factors…; full satisfaction for all workers is unlikely, though partial satisfaction is achievable

Lexis: wellbeing, promotion prospects, work-life balance, job security, intrinsic motivation

Grammar: While X contributes to…, it is unrealistic to expect…

Analysis insight: Q2 is the Band 7 discriminator. Strong answers qualify: possible for many, unrealistic for all.

Exam 3 — Technology & relationships

Prompt

Nowadays the way many people interact with each other has changed because of technology. In what ways has technology affected the types of relationships people make? Has this become a positive or negative development?

Task split

  • Q1: ways / effects on relationship types
  • Q2: positive/negative judgement

Trap analysis

  • Q1 becomes generic “technology is good/bad”.
  • Missing “types of relationships” (online friends, long-distance family, weaker local ties).
  • Q2 sits on the fence with no overall view.

Band 7 plan

Q1 pointsmore long-distance maintenance; broader weak ties via social media; sometimes less face-to-face depth
Q2 stancee.g. largely positive if balanced OR largely negative for community closeness
Supportone clear reason + example + short concession

Lexis: interact, maintain contact, weak ties, face-to-face interaction, sense of belonging

Must-have sentence for Q2: Overall, I would argue that this is largely a … development because…

Exam 4 — Foreign films vs local films

Prompt

Many people prefer to watch foreign films rather than locally produced films. Why do you think this is the case? Should governments give financial support to local film industries?

Task split

  • Q1: reasons
  • Q2: should (policy opinion)

Trap analysis

  • Q2 answered as pure culture essay without clear should/should not.
  • Extreme answers (“local films will die”) without mechanism.
  • No conditions on funding.

Band 7 plan

Q1higher budgets/effects; global stars & marketing; language/international appeal
Q2Conditional yes: protect cultural identity + quality incentives; avoid blank subsidies
ConcPrefer foreign due to…; governments should support selectively

Lexis: box-office, subsidies, cultural identity, production values, film industry

Analysis insight: Best Band 7 answers use conditional policy (should, but only if…).

Exam 5 — Advertising everywhere

Prompt

There is a lot of advertising today. Why is there so much advertising? Do you think it is a positive or negative development?

Task split

  • Q1: reasons
  • Q2: positive/negative

Trap analysis

  • Students only complain about ads (Q2) and skip commercial reasons (Q1).
  • No distinction between informative advertising and manipulative advertising.

Band 7 plan

Q1business competition; digital platforms make ads cheap/targeted; consumer culture
Q2largely negative if it encourages overconsumption; short concession: useful product information
Lexisconsumerism, targeted advertising, persuasive techniques, overconsumption, commercial pressure

Exam 6 — Print vs online reading

Prompt

In the future, nobody will buy printed newspapers or books because they will be able to read everything they want online. Why do some people think this? Is this a positive or negative development?

Task split

  • Q1: reasons for that prediction/belief
  • Q2: positive/negative

Trap analysis

  • Writing only about “I love paper books”.
  • Ignoring cost, convenience, environment, access arguments for Q1.

Band 7 plan

Q1cheaper access; convenience/portability; instant updates; less storage
Q2choose a side: e.g. mixed but concerning for deep reading / publishers OR positive for access
KeyAnswer why people think print will disappear, not only whether you agree it will

Exam 7 — Owning a home (Cambridge IELTS 16)

Prompt

In some countries, owning a home rather than renting one is very important for people. Why might this be the case? Do you think this is a positive or negative situation?

Task split

  • Q1: reasons
  • Q2: positive/negative situation

Trap analysis

  • Turning it into “how to buy a house”.
  • Q2 discusses only rich vs poor without clear overall judgement.

Band 7 plan

Q1financial security/asset; social status; stability for family; cultural expectation
Q2e.g. negative overall if it creates debt pressure & inequality; or positive for stability with concession
Lexismortgage, asset, financial security, housing market, social status, long-term investment

Analysis insight: Strong essays connect Q1 motives to Q2 social consequences (debt, delayed marriage, inequality).

Exam 11 — Ageing population

Exam 13 — Working from home

Prompt

An increasing number of people are working from home rather than in an office. Why is this happening? What effects does this have on individuals and society?

Task split

  • Q1: reasons
  • Q2: effects (not opinion; not solutions)

Trap analysis

  • Writing advantages/disadvantages as if asked outweigh.
  • Missing society-level effects (cities, transport, local business).

Band 7 plan

Q1technology/platforms; company cost-saving; work-life preference; post-pandemic norms
Q2individuals: flexibility vs isolation; society: less commuting pollution, pressure on city centres/cafés
Lexisremote work, productivity, social isolation, urban centres, digital infrastructure

Analysis insight: Q2 = effects, so avoid “governments should…”.

A. Task Analysis, Balance & Cohesion

A1. Identify Q1 / Q2 types (8 min)

For each prompt, label Q1 and Q2:

reasons · problems · effects/ways · solutions · opinion (positive/negative) · should

  1. People live longer. What problems does this cause? What can be done?
  2. Technology has changed relationships. In what ways? Is this positive or negative?
  3. Many prefer foreign films. Why? Should governments support local films?
  4. More people work from home. Why? What effects does this have on society?
  5. Children use smartphones early. Why is this happening? Do the advantages outweigh the disadvantages?

Answers — A1

  1. Q1 problems · Q2 solutions
  2. Q1 effects/ways · Q2 opinion
  3. Q1 reasons · Q2 should
  4. Q1 reasons · Q2 effects
  5. Q1 reasons · Q2 opinion (outweigh)

A2. Balance check (8 min)

Which essay plan is better for Band 7? Why?

Plan X

  • Intro
  • Long paragraph on problems only
  • Two sentences on solutions
  • Conclusion

Plan Y

  • Intro
  • Full paragraph on problems
  • Full paragraph on solutions
  • Conclusion covering both

Answer — A2

Plan Y — both questions are developed; Plan X under-answers Q2 and risks a TR ceiling at 6.5.

A3. Choose the best linker (10 min)

  1. Life expectancy is rising. (As a result / Although / For instance), pension systems face greater pressure.
  2. Technology helps friends stay in touch across countries. (However / Therefore / Due to), some local face-to-face contact may decline.
  3. Foreign films often have bigger budgets. (Because / For example / In order to), Hollywood productions attract global audiences with advanced effects.
  4. Governments can support local cinema. (This can be done by / Because of / Whereas) offering grants to high-quality local projects.
  5. Remote friendships are easier to maintain. (Overall / For instance / Despite), I would argue this is largely a positive development.

Answers — A3

  1. As a result
  2. However
  3. For example
  4. This can be done by
  5. Overall

A4. Write a Q1-only paragraph (15 min)

Prompt: What problems does an ageing population cause for society?

Write 90–120 words. Use at least 3 linkers. One central idea cluster only.

Teacher check

  • Answers Q1 only
  • Mechanism explained
  • Example present
  • No solutions mixed in

D. Grammar for Band 7

D1. Fix the collocation / article errors (10 min)

  1. One reason of this problem is technology.
  2. This is a solution of ageing society.
  3. Technology effect people’s relationships.
  4. Government should to support local films.
  5. The life expectancy is increasing in many country.
  6. There have many advantages.
  7. It bring negative effects to society.
  8. People prefer foreign films because of the better quality.

Answers — D1

  1. One reason for this problem… / The reason why…
  2. a solution to an ageing society
  3. Technology affects… / has an effect on…
  4. Governments should support… (delete to)
  5. Life expectancy is increasing in many countries. (usually no “the”)
  6. There are many advantages.
  7. It brings negative effects…
  8. OK / …because they have better quality.

  2. Technology connects families abroad. Local meet-ups decline. (while)
  3. Foreign films have bigger budgets. They dominate cinemas. (which)
  4. Governments fund local films. Cultural identity is protected. (so that)
  5. Online chatting is convenient. It may lack emotional depth. (although)

E. Mixed Upgrades

E1. Upgrade Q1 paragraph (ageing) (15 min)

6.5

Firstly, old people make many problems. They need money and doctors. Also society is stressed. So ageing is bad for country.

Tasks: remove mechanical linker · add mechanism · upgrade 4 words · keep Q1 only

Model Band 7 — E1

One major problem caused by rising life expectancy is increased pressure on public services. Because older citizens often need long-term healthcare, hospitals and care homes face heavier demand. At the same time, pension systems become harder to sustain when a smaller workforce supports a larger retired population. As a result, both individuals and society carry a greater financial burden.

E2. Upgrade Q2 paragraph (films / should) (15 min)

6.5

Secondly, government should give money to local films. This is important for culture. If not, local films will die. So I agree.

Tasks: make stance conditional · add who/how/result · avoid extreme die

Model Band 7 — E2

In my view, governments should provide financial support to local film industries, but the support should be targeted. Grants and tax incentives can help local producers compete with high-budget foreign studios and protect cultural identity. However, funding should reward quality and audience engagement rather than protect weak productions automatically. In this way, local cinema can develop sustainably.
