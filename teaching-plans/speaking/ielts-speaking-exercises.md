# IELTS Speaking Exercise Pack  
**Level:** Band 6.5 → 7.0–7.5  
**Parts:** 1 · 2 · 3  

Use in Lessons 2–7 or as 10–20 minute homework drills.  
Answer keys follow each section.

# A. Part 1 Extension & Fluency

## A1. Extender bank (8 min)

Match the starter to a natural extender.

| Starter | Extender |
|---|---|
| 1. Yes, I do. | a. especially on weekends when I have more free time |
| 2. Not really. | b. because my schedule is already quite full during the week |
| 3. It depends. | c. if I’m with friends I prefer cafés; alone I stay home |
| 4. Quite often. | d. I used to, but these days I rarely have the chance |
| 5. I used to. | e. though it can be tiring after a long day |

### Answers — A1
1-a/e · 2-b/d · 3-c · 4-a · 5-d  
**Preferred:** 1-a, 2-b, 3-c, 4-a (or e), 5-d

4. That museum was **interesting**.  
5. Public transport is **convenient**.

## A3. 3-beat frame fill (12 min)

Complete each answer with **Reason + Example**.

1. Q: Do you enjoy cooking?  
   A: Yes, I do. ________________. For example, ________________.

2. Q: How do you usually go to work/school?  
   A: I usually ________________. It’s practical because ________________.

3. Q: Do you prefer mornings or evenings?  
   A: I prefer ________________. I feel ________________. For instance, ________________.

4. Q: Are you a morning person?  
   A: Not really. ________________.

### Model answers — A3 (samples)
1. …It helps me relax after study. For example, I often cook simple pasta on Friday evenings.  
2. …take the metro. It’s practical because it’s faster than driving in rush hour.  
3. …evenings. I feel more focused then. For instance, I revise better after dinner.  
4. …I need time to wake up, so my best work usually happens later in the day.

---

## A4. Fluency repair (10 min)

Replace long silence with a short repair phrase, then continue.

Repair box:  
*What I mean is…* · *Let me think for a second…* · *The point I’m making is…* · *How can I put it…*

Practise aloud:
1. You forget the word “colleague”.  
2. You start, then lose your reason.  
3. You realise your example is weak.  
4. You need one second to choose an opinion.

### Model — A4
1. *A person I work with — what I mean is, a colleague…*  
2. *Let me think for a second… mainly because it saves time.*  
3. *How can I put it… a clearer example would be…*  
4. *The point I’m making is that it depends on the situation.*

---

## A5. Spoken linking (Part 1 & 3) (8 min)

Choose the most natural option for speech.

1. I like living here **(because / due to the fact that)** the air is cleaner.  
2. The city is crowded. **(Also / In addition to the aforementioned point,)** rents are high.  
3. I enjoy sport. **(But / However,)** I don’t get much time these days.  
4. Online classes are flexible. **(So / Therefore,)** many students prefer them.  
5. I’m not against technology. **(I mean / Whereby)** it just needs balance.

### Answers — A5
1. because *(more natural in Part 1)*  
2. Also  
3. But *(However also OK in Part 3)*  
4. So *(Therefore OK in Part 3)*  
5. I mean

# B. Part 2 Planning & Storytelling

## B1. 4-box notes (10 min)

For this card, write **only notes** (not sentences):

> Describe a skill you learned that is useful.  
> You should say: what the skill is · when you learned it · how you learned it · and why it is useful.

| Box | Notes |
|---|---|
| What | |
| When / Where | |
| How / Details | |
| Why / Feeling | |

**Teacher check:** no full sentences; max 12–15 words per box.

---

## B2. Opening lines bank (8 min)

Write one opening for each card type.

1. Person: ________________________________  
2. Place: ________________________________  
3. Object: ________________________________  
4. Event: ________________________________  
5. Skill: ________________________________

### Model — B2
1. I’d like to talk about a teacher who really influenced me.  
2. One place I enjoy visiting is a small riverside park near my home.  
3. The object I’d choose is an old watch my father gave me.  
4. I’m going to describe a graduation trip I took last year.  
5. A useful skill I learned is touch-typing / public speaking / cooking basic meals.

---

## B3. Ending / reflection lines (8 min)

Finish strongly (avoid sudden stop).

1. Looking back, ________________________________.  
2. What made it memorable was ________________________________.  
3. Even now, ________________________________.  
4. That’s why ________________________________.  
5. If I had the chance again, ________________________________.

### Model — B3
1. …I realise how much that experience boosted my confidence.  
2. …the people I shared it with, not just the place itself.  
3. …I still use that skill almost every day.  
4. …I wanted to talk about this topic.  
5. …I’d spend more time noticing the small details.

---

## B4. Story upgrade (12 min)

Upgrade this flat Part 2 extract:

> I went to a park. It was nice. I saw some people. I felt happy. Then I went home.

Add: time marker · specific detail · feeling · reflection.

### Model — B4
> Last spring I went to a riverside park near my flat after a stressful exam week. Families were having picnics, and the light on the water made the place feel calm. I remember sitting there for almost an hour and finally feeling my shoulders drop. Looking back, that short walk reminded me how much I need quiet outdoor spaces.

---

## B5. Narrative markers (8 min)

Put in a logical order, then say one 45-second story using all of them.

`at first` · `later on` · `after a while` · `eventually` · `looking back`

### Suggested order — B5
at first → later on / after a while → eventually → looking back

# C. Part 3 Lexis & Opinion Language

## C1. Opinion stems (8 min)

Complete with a clear view.

1. I’d say that ________________________________.  
2. To some extent, ________________________________.  
3. It really depends on ________________________________.  
4. A key reason is that ________________________________.  
5. I’m not entirely convinced that ________________________________.

Record yourself saying each once.

---

## C2. Develop the “why” (10 min)

Take each opinion and add **because + result**.

1. Cities are becoming more stressful.  
2. Online learning is useful.  
3. Young people change jobs more often.  
4. Public transport should be prioritised.  
5. Face-to-face friendship still matters.

### Model — C2
1. …because commuting and living costs are rising, so daily life feels more pressured.  
2. …because students can review recordings, which makes revision more flexible.  
3. …because they want faster growth, so they switch roles for better opportunities.  
4. …because roads are overcrowded; better transit would cut congestion and pollution.  
5. …because real conversations build trust more deeply than short online chats.

---

## C3. Brief both-sides (Part 3) (10 min)

Use: *On the one hand… On the other hand… Overall I think…*

Topic: Has technology improved education?

Speak 45–60 seconds.

### Model outline — C3
- On the one hand: access to resources, flexibility  
- On the other hand: distraction, weaker focus / less interaction  
- Overall: helpful as support, not a full replacement

# D. Spoken Grammar Accuracy

## D1. Tense control for Part 2 (10 min)

Correct and say aloud.

1. Yesterday I go to the museum and see a painting.  
2. While I was walk home, I meet my teacher.  
3. I have visit that place when I was a child.  
4. At first I don’t like the course, but later I enjoyed it.  
5. Looking back, it teach me a lot about teamwork.

### Answers — D1
1. went / saw  
2. walking / met  
3. visited *(or: I visited…)*  
4. didn’t like  
5. taught  

---

## D2. Spot & fix (common 6.5 errors) (10 min)

1. I very like my hometown.  
2. In my city have many parks.  
3. It depend on the person.  
4. She explain me the problem.  
5. I am agree with this idea.  
6. There have a lot of traffic.  
7. He don’t know the answer.  
8. I lost my way and ask a stranger.

### Answers — D2
1. I **really** like…  
2. In my city **there are** many parks.  
3. It **depends**…  
4. She **explained** the problem **to me**.  
5. I **agree**…  
6. There **is** a lot of traffic.  
7. He **doesn’t** know…  
8. …and **asked** a stranger.

---

## D3. Complex sentences for speaking (12 min)

Combine and speak naturally (not like writing).

1. I live near the centre. I can walk to work. *(so)*  
2. Technology is useful. It can waste time. *(although)*  
3. People move to big cities. They want better jobs. *(because)*  
4. Public transport improves. Fewer people drive. *(if)*  
5. I failed the first time. I tried again. *(even though)*

### Model — D3
1. I live near the centre, so I can walk to work.  
2. Although technology is useful, it can also waste time.  
3. People move to big cities because they want better jobs.  
4. If public transport improves, fewer people will drive.  
5. Even though I failed the first time, I tried again.

---

## D4. Accuracy under time (10 min)

Answer each in **20 seconds**. Then repeat and self-correct one grammar slip.

1. What do you dislike about public transport?  
2. Do children have enough free time nowadays?  
3. How has your city changed in recent years?  
4. Are local shops better than online shopping?  
5. What makes a good neighbour?

**Teacher check:** one repair is fine; long silence is not.

# E. Pronunciation / Stress / Chunking

## E1. Stress marking (10 min)

Underline the stressed syllable, then say each word twice.

`important` · `experience` · `development` · `comfortable` · `technology` · `education` · `opportunity` · `interesting`

### Answers — E1
im-**POR**-tant · ex-**PE**-*ri*-ence · de-**VE**lop-ment · **COM**-for-ta-ble · tech-**NO**-lo-gy · e-du-**CA**-tion · o-ppor-**TU**-ni-ty · **IN**-teres-ting  

---

## E2. Chunking (10 min)

Mark `/` for sense groups and read aloud.

1. I’d say online learning is useful / because it gives people more flexibility / especially if they work part-time.  
2. Looking back / that trip taught me to be more independent / and to handle unexpected problems calmly.

Now chunk this unmarked answer yourself:

3. My hometown is not very large but I like it because life is quieter and I can get to work quickly without too much stress.

### Model — E3 chunking
My hometown is not very large / but I like it / because life is quieter / and I can get to work quickly / without too much stress.

---

## E3. Clarity repair (10 min)

Record once, then re-record with clearer endings.

Focus words: `asked` `worked` `changed` `friends` `months` `public` `transport` `important`

Task: answer  
*Has your hometown changed much in recent years?*  
(30–40 seconds)

Checklist:
- [ ] past endings audible  
- [ ] final sounds not cut off  
- [ ] finished idea with falling tone

# Teacher Marking Shortcuts

Score homework recordings on only:
1. **Task length/shape** (Part 1/2/3 formula)  
2. **Clarity** (can understand without effort?)  
3. **Upgrade transfer** (did new language appear?)

If language is good but answers stay short, set a “minimum 3 sentences” rule for one week.
