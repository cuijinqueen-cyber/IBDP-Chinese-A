# IELTS Speaking Teaching Plan  
**Student level:** Band 6.5  
**Target:** Band 7.0–7.5  
**Focus:** Speaking Part 1 · Part 2 · Part 3

---

## 1. Course Snapshot

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Length** | 8 lessons × 90 minutes + daily micro-practice |
| **Format** | 1:1 tutoring |
| **Weekly load** | 2 lessons + 3 short speaking drills |
| **Assessment** | Diagnostic (L1) → Mid-mock (L5) → Final mock (L8) |
| **Success criteria** | Stable Band 7 features in Fluency & Coherence + Lexical Resource; no criterion below 6.5 |

### Why 6.5 speakers usually stall

| Criterion | Typical 6.5 pattern | Band 7–7.5 target |
|---|---|---|
| **Fluency & Coherence** | Can keep going, but hesitates on longer answers; overuses *and / because* | Speaks at length with natural pacing; clear organisation |
| **Lexical Resource** | Enough words for familiar topics; some repetition / awkward paraphrase | Flexible vocabulary; paraphrase; some less common items |
| **Grammatical Range & Accuracy** | Mix of simple/complex; errors don’t always block meaning | Frequent error-free sentences; wider range of structures |
| **Pronunciation** | Generally clear; limited control of stress/intonation | Clear throughout; uses stress/intonation to support meaning |

**Teaching priority**
1. Extend answers without memorised scripts (FC)  
2. Topic vocabulary + paraphrase (LR)  
3. Accurate complex grammar under pressure (GRA)  
4. Stress, chunking, and ending intonation (Pron)

## 4. Band 7 Answer Formulas (Teach Explicitly)

### Part 1 — 3-beat answer
**Direct answer → Reason / Detail → Example or feeling**  
Target length: **2–4 sentences** (not yes/no; not a speech).

> Q: Do you like your hometown?  
> A: Yes, I do. It’s a mid-sized city with a slower pace than the capital, so daily life feels less stressful. For example, I can cycle to most places in about fifteen minutes.

### Part 2 — 4-box plan (40–50 seconds planning)
1. **What / Who**  
2. **When / Where**  
3. **What happened / details**  
4. **Why it matters / how you felt**  
Speak for **1:40–2:00**. If time remains, add one more detail—not a new topic.

### Part 3 — IDEA frame
**I**nsight (opinion) → **D**etail (why) → **E**xample → **A**lternative / consequence  
Avoid one-sentence answers; avoid memorised mini-essays.

## 5. Detailed Lesson Plans

### Lesson 1 — Diagnostic & Target Setting (90 min)

**Aim:** Get a real band profile across all 4 criteria.

**Procedure**
1. **Warm-up (10 min)**  
   Myth check: *Do I need native-like accent?* *Should I memorise Part 2?* Correct early.
2. **Full diagnostic (15–18 min)**  
   Part 1 (4–5 min) → Part 2 (3–4 min incl. prep) → Part 3 (4–5 min). Record audio.
3. **Immediate self-review (15 min)**  
   Student listens to 2 answers and marks:
   - [ ] Too short / too long  
   - [ ] Repeated words  
   - [ ] Long silent pauses  
   - [ ] Unclear word endings
4. **Teacher profile (rest of lesson)**  
   Band estimate + **Top 3 actions only**.

**Homework**
- Re-answer 5 Part 1 questions from the mock (record on phone).  
- Start **Speaking Upgrade Log**: *Weak answer → Better answer → Useful phrase*.

---

### Lesson 2 — Part 1 Extension System (90 min)

**Aim:** Turn short answers into natural Band-7 replies.

**Topics:** Hometown · Work/Study · Free time · Daily routine

**Procedure**
1. **Model contrast (15 min)**  
   - 6.5: *Yes, I like it. It’s nice.*  
   - 7.0: *Yes, mostly. It’s convenient for work, and there are enough cafés and parks to unwind after a long day.*
2. **3-beat drilling (30 min)**  
   12 questions; student must hit Answer + Reason + Example.
3. **Paraphrase hotseat (20 min)**  
   Replace repeated *nice / good / interesting / convenient* with topic-specific words.
4. **Mini Part 1 mock (20 min)**  
   Record 4–5 minutes; quick feedback.
5. **Wrap (5 min)**

**In-class exercises:** A1 extender bank · A2 replace weak adjectives · A3 fill the frame · A4 fluency repair  

**Homework**
- Record 10 Part 1 answers (max 25 seconds each).  
- A5 linking for speech.

---

### Lesson 3 — Part 2 Architecture (90 min)

**Aim:** Install a reliable 1-minute plan + 2-minute talk.

**Cue cards (examples)**
- Describe a person who inspired you  
- Describe a place you enjoy visiting  
- Describe an object that is important to you

**Procedure**
1. **Planning template (20 min)**  
   Teach 4-box notes (not full sentences).
2. **Live plan races (15 min)**  
   3 cards × 1 minute planning only.
3. **Guided talk (25 min)**  
   One full Part 2 with mid-talk prompts if student freezes: *Where exactly? What happened next? Why memorable?*
4. **Second card timed (20 min)**  
   No teacher rescue.
5. **Checklist (10 min)**  
   Timing, coverage of bullet points, clear ending.

**In-class exercises:** B1 note-taking · B2 opening lines · B3 ending lines  

**Homework**
- 3 Part 2 recordings (different cards).  
- Transcribe 30 seconds of the weakest one.

---

### Lesson 4 — Part 2 Storytelling Upgrade (90 min)

**Aim:** Improve narrative flow and detail quality.

**Focus language**
- Time markers: *at first, later on, eventually, looking back*  
- Detail upgrades: senses, people’s reactions, personal meaning  
- Ending: reflection, not sudden stop

**Procedure**
1. Feedback on homework recordings (20 min)  
2. Story spine practice (25 min): beginning → complication → outcome → reflection  
3. Upgrade a flat story (20 min)  
4. Timed Part 2 (20 min)  
5. Micro pronunciation of past forms (5 min): *-ed* endings

**In-class exercises:** B4 story upgrades · B5 past narrative grammar · D1 tense control  

**Homework**
- Re-record the upgraded story.  
- Learn 8 narrative markers and use each once.

---

### Lesson 5 — Part 3 Opinion & Explanation (90 min)

**Aim:** Produce longer analytical answers without freezing.

**Topic link:** Education / Technology (from Part 2 themes)

**Sample Part 3 stems**
- Why do some people prefer online learning?  
- Has technology improved education or made it worse?  
- What skills will be most important for future jobs?

**Procedure**
1. **Compare Part 1 vs Part 3 (10 min)**  
   Part 3 needs broader view + support.
2. **IDEA frame drilling (30 min)**  
   8 questions; enforce Insight → Detail → Example → Alternative.
3. **Agree / partly agree language (20 min)**  
   *To some extent… / I’d say it depends… / A key reason is…*
4. **Linked Part 2→3 mock (25 min)**  
5. **Wrap (5 min)**

**In-class exercises:** C1 opinion stems · C2 develop the why · C3 discuss both sides briefly · A5 discourse markers  

**Homework**
- Record 8 Part 3 answers (40–60 seconds each).  
- C4 topic phrase bank (education/tech).

---

### Lesson 6 — Lexis & Grammar Lift for Speaking (90 min)

**Aim:** Reduce repeated errors; expand flexible vocabulary.

**Common 6.5 speaking traps**
- *I very like* → *I really like*  
- *In my country have…* → *In my country there is/are…*  
- *It depend* → *It depends*  
- Overuse of *something like that / you know*  
- Translating word order from L1

**Procedure**
1. Upgrade Log review (20 min)  
2. Paraphrase ladder (25 min): simple → precise → flexible  
3. High-value grammar for speech (25 min): conditionals, relative clauses, comparatives  
4. Weak-topic repair (20 min): student’s hardest Part 3 topic

**In-class exercises:** C5 paraphrase · D2 spot & fix · D3 complex spoken sentences · D4 accuracy under time  

**Homework**
- 15-minute grammar-to-speech drill.  
- Re-answer Lesson 1’s weakest 5 questions.

---

### Lesson 7 — Pronunciation, Stress & Chunking (90 min)

**Aim:** Make meaning clearer through sound, not “accent training”.

**Focus**
- Word stress in key content words  
- Chunking: speak in sense groups  
- Falling tone for finished ideas; rising for lists/uncertainty  
- Problem sounds personal to the student (identify from recordings)

**Procedure**
1. Shadow 4 model short answers (20 min)  
2. Mark stress on student’s own Part 2 script (20 min)  
3. Chunked re-delivery (25 min)  
4. Mixed mini-mock with pronunciation targets (20 min)  
5. Personal sound list (5 min)

**In-class exercises:** E1 stress marking · E2 chunking · E3 clarity repair  

**Homework**
- Shadow 10 minutes daily.  
- Re-record one Part 2 focusing only on clarity and ending.

---

### Lesson 8 — Final Mock & Feedback Conference (90 min)

**Aim:** Confirm readiness and set maintenance plan.

**Procedure**
1. Full mock under exam timing (20 min) + recording  
2. Criterion-by-criterion conference (50 min)  
3. Final 2-week plan (20 min):
   - Daily: 10 Part 1 OR 1 Part 2 OR 5 Part 3  
   - Every other day: listen + upgrade one weak answer  
   - 2 full mocks before test day

## 7. Band 7 Checklists

### Part 1
- [ ] Direct answer first  
- [ ] One reason + one detail/example  
- [ ] 2–4 sentences  
- [ ] No memorised speech

### Part 2
- [ ] Notes in 4 boxes, not full script  
- [ ] Cover all bullet points  
- [ ] Speak 1:40–2:00  
- [ ] Clear ending / reflection

### Part 3
- [ ] Opinion is clear  
- [ ] Explanation is developed  
- [ ] Example or comparison included  
- [ ] Not memorised essay language

### Always
- [ ] Repair quickly if stuck (*What I mean is…*)  
- [ ] Avoid long silence; paraphrase instead  
- [ ] Check *he/she/it + verb* and countable nouns

## 8. Ready-to-Use Question Sets

### Set A — Hometown / City life
**Part 1:** Where is your hometown? What do you like about it? Has it changed?  
**Part 2:** Describe a place in your city you often go to.  
**Part 3:** Why do people move to big cities? What problems do growing cities cause? How can cities become more liveable?

### Set B — Work / Study
**Part 1:** Do you work or study? What do you like about it? What is difficult?  
**Part 2:** Describe a skill you learned that is useful for work or study.  
**Part 3:** What skills are most valued by employers today? Is university still necessary? How will AI change jobs?

### Set C — Free time / People
**Part 1:** What do you do in your free time? Do you prefer alone time or with friends?  
**Part 2:** Describe a person you enjoy spending time with.  
**Part 3:** Have leisure habits changed with technology? Are friendships different now from the past?

## 11. Teacher Script (Lesson 2 opening)

> “Today we fix Part 1. The goal is not longer memorised answers.  
> The goal is a natural three-beat reply: answer, reason, example.  
> If you can do that automatically, Part 3 becomes much easier later.”

---

## 12. Tutor Notes

1. **Record every key performance** — speaking progress is audible.  
2. **Mark less:** band profile + Top 3 actions.  
3. **Ban full Part 2 scripts**; allow bullet notes only.  
4. Push **transfer**: phrases from exercises must appear in the next mock.  
5. Aim for **stable Band 7 habits**, not one perfect recording.
