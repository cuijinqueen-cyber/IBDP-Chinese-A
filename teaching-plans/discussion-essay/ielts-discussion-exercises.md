# A. Linking Words & Cohesion (Discussion Essays)

## A1. Sort the linkers (5 min)

Put each linker into the correct group.

`however` · `while` · `for instance` · `in contrast` · `as a result` · `although` · `on the other hand` · `therefore` · `for example` · `whereas` · `nevertheless` · `specifically` · `furthermore` · `despite this` · `in addition`

| Purpose | Linkers |
|---|---|
| Add an idea | |
| Show contrast / concession | |
| Show result | |
| Give an example | |

### Answers — A1
- **Add:** furthermore, in addition  
- **Contrast/concession:** however, while, in contrast, although, on the other hand, whereas, nevertheless, despite this  
- **Result:** as a result, therefore  
- **Example:** for instance, for example, specifically  

---

## A2. Choose the best linker (10 min)

1. Competition can motivate children. **(However / Therefore / For example)**, it may also create unnecessary stress.  
2. Co-operation prepares young people for teamwork. **(Whereas / As a result / For instance)**, they often adapt better in the workplace.  
3. Online platforms are convenient. **(Although / Therefore / In addition)** theatres are less convenient, many people still value the live atmosphere.  
4. Streaming is cheap and flexible. **(In contrast / Furthermore / Specifically)**, cinemas create shared cultural experiences.  
5. Some students enjoy competing. **(Despite this / For example / Consequently)**, collaboration remains more useful in most careers.

### Answers — A2
1. However  
2. As a result  
3. Although  
4. In contrast  
5. Despite this  

---

## A3. Replace mechanical discussion phrases (10 min)

Rewrite without using: *Firstly, Secondly, On the one hand, On the other hand, In conclusion.*

1. On the one hand, competition makes children work harder.  
2. On the other hand, co-operation is more useful for adult life.  
3. Firstly, online entertainment is cheaper.  
4. Secondly, theatres still support local jobs.  
5. In conclusion, both views are important but co-operation is better.

### Model answers — A3
1. One argument in favour of competition is that it can make children work harder.  
2. A stronger point is that co-operation is more useful for adult life.  
3. A common argument is that online entertainment is cheaper.  
4. Equally important, theatres still support local jobs.  
5. Overall, both views have value, but co-operation should take priority.

## B5. Gap-fill paragraph — competition vs co-operation (10 min)

`although` · `collaboration` · `priority` · `competitive` · `teamwork` · `useful` · `motivation` · `interpersonal`

__________ competition can increase children’s __________, I believe co-operation should take __________. In adult life, most workplaces value __________ and __________ skills more than individual victory. For example, employees are often assessed on __________ rather than on who beats colleagues. As a result, teaching children to work with others is more __________ than focusing only on a __________ mindset.

### Answers — B5
**Although** competition can increase children’s **motivation**, I believe co-operation should take **priority**. In adult life, most workplaces value **teamwork** and **interpersonal** skills more than individual victory. For example, employees are often assessed on **collaboration** rather than on who beats colleagues. As a result, teaching children to work with others is more **useful** than focusing only on a **competitive** mindset.

## C4. Gap-fill paragraph — theatres/cinemas (10 min)

`economically` · `streaming` · `shared` · `culturally` · `convenience` · `replace` · `revenue` · `although` · `however`

__________ online entertainment offers great __________, I do not believe it can fully __________ theatres and cinemas. __________ platforms allow people to watch films cheaply at home; __________, traditional venues still matter __________ and __________. They generate __________ and jobs, and they provide a __________ cultural experience that home viewing cannot copy exactly.

### Answers — C4
**Although** online entertainment offers great **convenience**, I do not believe it can fully **replace** theatres and cinemas. **Streaming** platforms allow people to watch films cheaply at home; **however**, traditional venues still matter **economically** and **culturally**. They generate **revenue** and jobs, and they provide a **shared** cultural experience that home viewing cannot copy exactly.

# D. Grammar for Band 7

## D1. Articles & countable nouns (10 min)

Correct each sentence.

1. Competition can build the confidence in child.  
2. Co-operation is important in workplace.  
3. The online entertainments are very convenient.  
4. Theatre play an important cultural role.  
5. Children should learn teamwork skill.  
6. Going to cinema is expensive for some family.  
7. This is useful advice for the students.  
8. Digital age has changed how we watch films.

### Answers — D1
1. Competition can build confidence in **a child / children**. *(or: build **the** confidence of a child)*  
2. Co-operation is important in **the** workplace.  
3. Online **entertainment is** very convenient.  
4. **Theatres play** / **Theatre plays** an important cultural role.  
5. Children should learn teamwork **skills**.  
6. Going to **the** cinema is expensive for some **families**.  
7. This is useful advice for students. *(no “the” needed)*  
8. **The** digital age has changed how we watch films.

## D2. Complex sentences — combine (12 min)

Combine using the word in brackets.

1. Competition can motivate children. It may also cause stress. *(although)*  
2. Children learn to co-operate. They become more useful at work. *(if)*  
3. Streaming is convenient. Many people still value live performance. *(while)*  
4. Theatres create jobs. They also support cultural life. *(which)*  
5. Online platforms are cheaper. They cannot replace the cinema atmosphere. *(even though)*  
6. Schools encourage only winning. Pupils may struggle in teams later. *(when)*

### Model answers — D2
1. Although competition can motivate children, it may also cause stress.  
2. If children learn to co-operate, they become more useful at work.  
3. While streaming is convenient, many people still value live performance.  
4. Theatres create jobs, which also supports / and they also support cultural life.  
   **Best:** Theatres create jobs, which helps local economies, and they also support cultural life.  
   **Simple accurate:** Theatres create jobs and support cultural life, which makes them socially valuable.  
5. Even though online platforms are cheaper, they cannot replace the cinema atmosphere.  
6. When schools encourage only winning, pupils may struggle in teams later.

# E. Mixed “6.5 → 7” Paragraph Upgrades

## E1. Upgrade this discussion paragraph (15 min)

**Band 6.5 version**
> On the one hand, competition is good. Children want to be better than others. It gives confidence. Also, they need it for job interviews. So schools should encourage it.

**Tasks**
1. Remove mechanical openers.  
2. Add explanation.  
3. Upgrade 4 vocabulary items.  
4. Use 2 complex sentences.

### Model Band 7 version — E1
> One reason to encourage competition is that it can raise children’s motivation. When pupils try to outperform their classmates, they often work harder and make faster progress. Success in contests may also build self-confidence. In addition, because adult life includes competitive situations such as job interviews, early experience of competition can help young people prepare for future challenges.

---

## E2. Upgrade this theatres/cinemas paragraph (15 min)

**Band 6.5 version**
> Some people say theatres are not important now because people watch everything online. It is cheaper and easier. So theatres and cinemas are useless in digital age.

**Tasks**
1. Soften extreme language (*useless*).  
2. Add precise vocabulary.  
3. Keep it as View A only (not your full opinion yet).  
4. Use one concession-ready ending.

### Model Band 7 version — E2
> Some people argue that theatres and cinemas have become less necessary in the digital age. Streaming platforms allow audiences to access a huge range of films and performances cheaply and conveniently at home. As a result, traditional venues may seem less essential for everyday entertainment than they were in the past.
