**Level:** Band 6.5 → 7.0–7.5  
**Topics:** Science aims · Space vs public services · Urbanisation line graph  

Use these exercises in Lessons 2–6, or as 15–20 minute homework drills.  
**Answer key** is at the end of each section.

# A. Linking Words & Cohesion

## A1. Sort the linkers (5 min)

Put each linker into the correct group.

`however` · `therefore` · `for instance` · `in addition` · `although` · `as a result` · `in contrast` · `specifically` · `while` · `furthermore` · `on the other hand` · `consequently` · `for example` · `despite this` · `whereas`

| Purpose | Linkers |
|---|---|
| Add an idea | |
| Show contrast / concession | |
| Show result | |
| Give an example | |

### Answers — A1
- **Add:** in addition, furthermore  
- **Contrast/concession:** however, although, in contrast, while, on the other hand, despite this, whereas  
- **Result:** therefore, as a result, consequently  
- **Example:** for instance, specifically, for example  

---

## A2. Choose the best linker (Band 7, not Band 6) (10 min)

Circle the most natural option.

1. Space projects can create new technologies. **(Moreover / However / For example)**, many citizens see little direct benefit in daily life.  
2. Public hospitals serve the whole population. **(Therefore / Whereas / For instance)**, governments should prioritise them over prestige projects.  
3. Science should improve people’s lives. **(In contrast / Although / As a result)**, curiosity-driven research can still be valuable.  
4. Malaysia’s urban population rose sharply. **(In addition / By contrast / Consequently)**, Thailand’s figure remained much lower for several decades.  
5. Basic research may seem impractical. **(Despite this / Furthermore / Specifically)**, it often leads to medical breakthroughs later.

### Answers — A2
1. However  
2. Therefore  
3. Although  
4. By contrast  
5. Despite this

## A3. Replace mechanical linkers (10 min)

Rewrite each sentence. Do **not** use: *Firstly, Secondly, Moreover, In conclusion.*

1. Firstly, space programmes are extremely expensive.  
2. Moreover, ordinary people do not benefit from them.  
3. Secondly, schools and hospitals help everyone.  
4. Moreover, better education can reduce poverty.  
5. In conclusion, governments should spend on public services.

**Model answers — A3**
1. One major concern is that space programmes are extremely expensive.  
2. Equally important, ordinary people rarely benefit from them directly.  
3. A stronger argument is that schools and hospitals help everyone.  
4. Better education can also reduce poverty over time.  
5. Overall, governments should prioritise spending on public services.

## A4. Build a cohesive paragraph (15 min)

Use **at least 4** linkers from the box. Do not start every sentence with a linker.

`although` · `as a result` · `for instance` · `in addition` · `however` · `therefore`

**Prompt:** Write one body paragraph (90–120 words) agreeing that public services deserve more funding than space programmes.

**Teacher check**
- [ ] Linkers are accurate  
- [ ] Not every sentence starts with a linker  
- [ ] Ideas are connected, not listed  
- [ ] Position is clear

1. Malaysia started at a similar level to the Philippines in 1970; __________, it rose much faster after 1990.

3. Thailand remained relatively low for many years, __________ accelerating after 2020.

## B5. Gap-fill paragraph — Science essay (10 min)

Complete with words from the box.

`wellbeing` · `secondary` · `breakthroughs` · `overarching` · `alleviate` · `applications` · `ethical` · `largely`

I __________ agree that the __________ aim of science should be to improve human __________. Because scientific research is often publicly funded, it has an __________ responsibility to __________ suffering and raise living standards. Medical __________ and clean-energy technologies are clear examples of work with practical __________. Other goals, such as national prestige, should remain __________.

### Answers — B5
I **largely** agree that the **overarching** aim of science should be to improve human **wellbeing**. Because scientific research is often publicly funded, it has an **ethical** responsibility to **alleviate** suffering and raise living standards. Medical **breakthroughs** and clean-energy technologies are clear examples of work with practical **applications**. Other goals, such as national prestige, should remain **secondary**.

## C2. Paraphrase the question (10 min)

Original:  
*The graph below gives information about the percentage of the population in four Asian countries living in cities from 1970 to 2020, with predictions for 2030 and 2040.*

Write **3 different paraphrases**. Do not copy more than 3 words in a row from the original.

**Model answers — C2**
1. The line graph illustrates the proportion of city residents in four Asian nations between 1970 and 2020, together with forecasts for 2030 and 2040.  
2. The chart shows how urban populations changed in the Philippines, Malaysia, Thailand and Indonesia from 1970 to 2020, and estimates figures for the following two decades.  
3. The graph compares the percentage of people living in urban areas in four Asian countries over a seventy-year period, including projections to 2040.

## C3. Data language upgrade (10 min)

Rewrite in Task 1 academic style.

1. Malaysia went up a lot.  
2. Thailand was the lowest for a long time.  
3. Indonesia will be about 65% in 2040.  
4. The Philippines was higher than Malaysia at the start, then Malaysia became higher.  
5. All countries will grow.

### Model answers — C3
1. Malaysia rose sharply / increased significantly.  
2. Thailand remained the least urbanised for much of the period.  
3. Indonesia is projected to reach around 65% by 2040.  
4. The Philippines started above Malaysia, but was later overtaken.  
5. All four countries are expected to see further growth.

## C4. Overview gap-fill (8 min)

`projected` · `overtake` · `proportion` · `fluctuated` · `highest` · `comparatively`

Overall, the __________ of people living in cities is __________ to rise in all four countries. Malaysia is expected to have the __________ urban population by 2040, while Thailand is likely to remain __________ lower. Indonesia climbs strongly and is forecast to __________ the Philippines, whose figure __________ before recovering.

### Answers — C4
Overall, the **proportion** of people living in cities is **projected** to rise in all four countries. Malaysia is expected to have the **highest** urban population by 2040, while Thailand is likely to remain **comparatively** lower. Indonesia climbs strongly and is forecast to **overtake** the Philippines, whose figure **fluctuated** before recovering.

## C5. Avoid repetition (10 min)

The paragraph below repeats *increase* too much. Rewrite with varied verbs/phrases.

> The percentage of people in cities increased in Malaysia. It also increased in Indonesia. Thailand increased slowly at first and then increased faster. The Philippines increased, then fell, and then increased again.

**Model rewrite — C5**

# D. Grammar for Band 7

## D1. Articles & countable nouns (10 min)

Correct each sentence.

1. Science should improve life of people.  
2. The space programmes require expensive equipments.  
3. Government should invest more in education.  
4. An useful example is medical research.  
5. Many informations about urbanisation are shown in the graph.  
6. Public service are essential for society.  
7. Malaysia had highest percentage in 2040.  
8. Research can have a positive impacts on health.

### Answers — D1
1. Science should improve **the lives of people** / **people’s lives**.  
2. Space programmes require expensive **equipment**. *(no “the” required unless specific)*  
3. **The / A** government should invest more in education. *(or: Governments should…)*  
4. **A** useful example is medical research.  
5. **Much information** about urbanisation **is** shown in the graph.  
6. Public **services** are essential for society.  
7. Malaysia had **the** highest percentage in 2040.  
8. Research can have **a positive impact** on health. / **positive impacts**

## D2. Complex sentences — combine (12 min)

Combine each pair into **one** accurate complex sentence. Use the word in brackets.

1. Space missions are impressive. They often bring limited benefits to ordinary citizens. *(although)*  
2. Governments underfund hospitals. Public health declines. *(if)*  
3. Science is publicly funded. It should prioritise human wellbeing. *(since / because)*  
4. Malaysia started at around 30%. It is projected to exceed 80%. *(which)*  
5. Pure research seems impractical. It can produce useful applications later. *(even though)*  
6. Thailand remained low for decades. Growth accelerated after 2020. *(before)*

### Model answers — D2
1. Although space missions are impressive, they often bring limited benefits to ordinary citizens.  
2. If governments underfund hospitals, public health declines.  
3. Since science is publicly funded, it should prioritise human wellbeing.  
4. Malaysia, which started at around 30%, is projected to exceed 80%.  
5. Even though pure research seems impractical, it can produce useful applications later.  
6. Thailand remained low for decades before growth accelerated after 2020.

## D3. Error correction — student-style paragraph (12 min)

Find and correct **10** errors.

> I strong agree that most important aim of science should be improve people’s life. Although some research are done for curiosity, but it should still help society. For example, medical breakthrough can reduce the sufferings. If science only focus on prestige, ordinary people will not get benefit. Therefore, government need to support researches that enhance wellbeing.

### Answers — D3 (corrected version)
> I **strongly** agree that **the** most important aim of science should be **to** improve people’s **lives**. Although some research **is** done for curiosity, it should still help society. *(delete “but”)* For example, medical **breakthroughs** can reduce **suffering**. If science only **focuses** on prestige, ordinary people will not **benefit**. Therefore, **governments / the government** need**s** to support **research** that enhance**s** wellbeing.

**Error list:** strong→strongly; missing *the*; be improve→be to improve; life→lives; research are→research is; although…but; breakthrough→breakthroughs; the sufferings→suffering; focus→focuses; get benefit→benefit; government need→government(s) need(s); researches→research; enhance→enhances (if research singular).

2. …public health and social stability may deteriorate.

## D5. Task 1 tense control (10 min)

Put the verb in the correct form.

1. In 1970, the Philippines __________ (have) the highest figure.  
2. Between 1990 and 2010, the Philippine rate __________ (fluctuate).  
3. Malaysia __________ (rise) sharply over the whole period.  
4. By 2040, Indonesia __________ (be projected / reach) around 65%.  
5. Thailand __________ (remain) comparatively low until 2020, after which it __________ (accelerate).

### Answers — D5
1. had  
2. fluctuated  
3. rose / increased  
4. is projected to reach  
5. remained; accelerated / is expected to accelerate *(depending on whether referring to past trend after 2020 prediction — teach: past for 1970–2020, prediction language for 2030–2040)*

**Teacher note for item 5:**  
- Historical: *remained… accelerated* (if treating post-2020 as shown trend/prediction carefully)  
- Safer Band 7: *remained comparatively low until 2020, after which it is expected to accelerate.*

# E. Mixed “6.5 → 7” Paragraph Upgrades

## E1. Upgrade this Task 2 paragraph (15 min)

**Band 6.5 version**
> Firstly, space programmes waste money. They need scientists and equipment. Also, they are dangerous. For example, Challenger. Moreover, normal people do not get benefits. Therefore, I think governments should stop this spending.

**Tasks for student**
1. Remove mechanical linkers.  
2. Add explanation (not only lists).  
3. Upgrade 5 vocabulary items.  
4. Use 2 complex sentences.  
5. Keep one clear central idea.

### Model Band 7 version — E1
> One strong reason to prioritise public services is that space programmes often deliver limited immediate value to ordinary citizens. Although such projects can be technologically impressive, they require enormous funding for facilities, research staff and equipment, and failure can waste public money. The Challenger disaster also illustrated the human risks involved. As a result, many taxpayers contribute to schemes from which they see little direct benefit, while local schools and hospitals remain under-resourced.

## E2. Upgrade this Task 1 paragraph (15 min)

**Band 6.5 version**
> Malaysia increased from 1970 to 2040. Indonesia also increased. Thailand increased slowly. Philippines increased then decreased then increased. Malaysia will be highest.

**Tasks**
1. Add comparisons.  
2. Add 4 precise data points (approx. is OK).  
3. Vary trend verbs.  
4. Use prediction language for future years.

### Model Band 7 version — E2
> Malaysia rose sharply from around 30% in 1970 and is projected to exceed 80% by 2040, the highest of the four countries. Indonesia also climbed steeply from roughly 12–15% and is expected to reach about 65%, overtaking both Thailand and the Philippines. By contrast, Thailand remained comparatively low for decades before accelerating towards 50%, while the Philippines fluctuated after 1990 and is forecast to finish near the mid-50% range.

# Teacher Marking Shortcuts

When checking exercise homework, score only:

1. **Accuracy** (grammar/vocab used correctly?)  
2. **Transfer** (did the student reuse items in their next essay?)  
3. **Naturalness** (does it sound forced?)

If accuracy is high but transfer is low, require a 4-sentence “reuse paragraph” before the next lesson.
