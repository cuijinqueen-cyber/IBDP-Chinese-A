# A. Linking Words（原因 / 方案文）

## A1. 分类（5 min）

`because` · `as a result` · `for instance` · `however` · `therefore` · `one solution is` · `due to` · `furthermore` · `this can be tackled by` · `for example` · `consequently` · `although` · `in order to` · `another measure is` · `since`

| 功能 | 词语 |
|---|---|
| 引出原因 | |
| 引出结果 | |
| 引出方案 | |
| 举例 | |
| 转折/让步 | |
| 添加信息 | |

### Answers — A1
- **原因：** because, due to, since  
- **结果：** as a result, therefore, consequently  
- **方案：** one solution is, this can be tackled by, another measure is, in order to  
- **举例：** for instance, for example  
- **转折/让步：** however, although  
- **添加：** furthermore  

---

## A2. 选最合适连接（10 min）

1. Many jobs are now sedentary. **(As a result / Although / For example)**, average fitness levels are falling.  
2. Junk food is cheap and convenient. **(Due to / Therefore / In order to)** busy lifestyles, families rely on it more often.  
3. Public transport is unreliable in some cities. **(However / This can be tackled by / Because)** investing in frequent bus and metro services.  
4. Doctors leave poorer countries for higher pay. **(For instance / Consequently / Although)**, rural hospitals face severe staff shortages.  
5. Congestion charging may be unpopular. **(Although / As a result / Another measure is)** it can reduce peak-hour traffic.

### Answers — A2
1. As a result  
2. Due to *(sentence may need slight rewrite: Due to busy lifestyles, …)*  
3. This can be tackled by  
4. Consequently  
5. Although

## A3. 去掉机械连接（10 min）

不要使用：*Firstly, Secondly, Thirdly, In conclusion.*

1. Firstly, people do less physical activity.  
2. Secondly, they eat too much fast food.  
3. Firstly, the government should improve hospitals’ salaries.  
4. Secondly, countries can offer scholarships with return contracts.  
5. In conclusion, better transport and fewer private cars can reduce congestion.

### Model answers — A3
1. One major cause is that people do less physical activity.  
2. A second factor is the overconsumption of fast food.  
3. One effective measure is to improve salaries for hospital staff.  
4. Countries could also offer scholarships with return contracts.  
5. Overall, better transport and reduced car use can ease congestion.

## B5. 完型填空 — 肥胖真题段（10 min）

`sedentary` · `measures` · `intake` · `campaigns` · `processed` · `tackled` · `fitness` · `as a result`

One major cause of rising body weight is a __________ lifestyle combined with a high __________ of ultra-__________ food. __________, average __________ levels have declined in many countries. This problem can be __________ through school and community sports programmes, clearer food labelling, and public health __________. Such __________ are more effective than simply telling people to “be healthier.”

### Answers — B5
One major cause of rising body weight is a **sedentary** lifestyle combined with a high **intake** of ultra-**processed** food. **As a result**, average **fitness** levels have declined in many countries. This problem can be **tackled** through school and community sports programmes, clearer food labelling, and public health **campaigns**. Such **measures** are more effective than simply telling people to “be healthier.”

## C4. 完型 — 人才外流（10 min）

`shortages` · `retention` · `brain drain` · `services` · `incentives` · `workforce` · `consequently` · `solutions`

__________ occurs when a large part of the skilled __________ leaves poorer countries. __________, schools and hospitals suffer staff __________ and public __________ decline. Possible __________ include better local pay, career development, and return __________. Without stronger __________ policies, the cycle is likely to continue.

### Answers — C4
**Brain drain** occurs when a large part of the skilled **workforce** leaves poorer countries. **Consequently**, schools and hospitals suffer staff **shortages** and public **services** decline. Possible **solutions** include better local pay, career development, and return **incentives**. Without stronger **retention** policies, the cycle is likely to continue.

## D2. 合并成复杂句（12 min）

2. Public transport improves. Fewer people drive. *(if)*  
3. Salaries remain low. Teachers keep leaving. *(unless)*

# E. Mixed “6.5 → 7” Upgrades

## E1. 升级原因段（真题 A）（15 min）

**6.5 原文**
> Firstly, people eat fast food. It is delicious. Also they don’t play sport. So they are fat and not healthy. The government should care about this problem.

**任务**
1. 去掉机械连接  
2. 写清因果机制  
3. 升级 5 个词汇  
4. 方案句先删掉（本段只写原因）  
5. 至少 2 个复杂句

### Model Band 7 — E1
> One major cause of rising body weight is the combination of energy-dense diets and physical inactivity. Because ultra-processed food is cheap and widely advertised, many families consume it regularly. At the same time, sedentary work and screen-based leisure reduce daily energy expenditure. As a result, average weight increases while fitness levels decline.

---

## E2. 升级方案段（真题 C）（15 min）

**6.5 原文**
> Secondly, government must solve traffic. They should make better buses. People should not drive cars. Then the problem will finish.

**任务**
1. 具体化 who + how + result  
2. 避免 *must solve / will finish*  
3. 加入一个可执行措施（如 congestion charging）

### Model Band 7 — E2
> This problem can be tackled by making public transport more reliable and affordable, so that fewer residents depend on private cars. Cities could also introduce congestion charging during peak hours, which discourages unnecessary driving and raises funds for metro and bus improvements. Over time, these measures would ease traffic pressure and shorten commuting times.
