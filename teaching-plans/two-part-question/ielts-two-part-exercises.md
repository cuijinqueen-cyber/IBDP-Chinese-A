# A. Task Analysis, Balance & Cohesion

## A1. Identify Q1 / Q2 types (8 min)

For each prompt, label Q1 and Q2:
`reasons` · `problems` · `effects/ways` · `solutions` · `opinion (positive/negative)` · `should`

1. People live longer. What problems does this cause? What can be done?  
2. Technology has changed relationships. In what ways? Is this positive or negative?  
3. Many prefer foreign films. Why? Should governments support local films?  
4. More people work from home. Why? What effects does this have on society?  
5. Children use smartphones early. Why is this happening? Do the advantages outweigh the disadvantages?

### Answers — A1
1. Q1 problems · Q2 solutions  
2. Q1 effects/ways · Q2 opinion  
3. Q1 reasons · Q2 should  
4. Q1 reasons · Q2 effects  
5. Q1 reasons · Q2 opinion (outweigh)

---

## A2. Balance check (8 min)

Which essay plan is better for Band 7? Why?

**Plan X**
- Intro  
- Long paragraph on problems only  
- Two sentences on solutions  
- Conclusion

**Plan Y**
- Intro  
- Full paragraph on problems  
- Full paragraph on solutions  
- Conclusion covering both

### Answer — A2
**Plan Y** — both questions are developed; Plan X under-answers Q2 and risks a TR ceiling at 6.5.

## A3. Choose the best linker (10 min)

1. Life expectancy is rising. **(As a result / Although / For instance)**, pension systems face greater pressure.  
2. Technology helps friends stay in touch across countries. **(However / Therefore / Due to)**, some local face-to-face contact may decline.  
3. Foreign films often have bigger budgets. **(Because / For example / In order to)**, Hollywood productions attract global audiences with advanced effects.  
4. Governments can support local cinema. **(This can be done by / Because of / Whereas)** offering grants to high-quality local projects.  
5. Remote friendships are easier to maintain. **(Overall / For instance / Despite)**, I would argue this is largely a positive development.

### Answers — A3
1. As a result  
2. However  
3. For example  
4. This can be done by  
5. Overall

## B5. Gap-fill — ageing two-part paragraph (10 min)

`expectancy` · `pension` · `alleviate` · `long-term` · `ageing` · `preventive` · `pressure` · `community`

Rising life __________ means many countries now have an __________ population. One major problem is increased __________ on __________ healthcare and __________ systems. To __________ these challenges, governments can expand __________ care services and promote __________ healthcare, while individuals maintain healthier lifestyles.

### Answers — B5
Rising life **expectancy** means many countries now have an **ageing** population. One major problem is increased **pressure** on **long-term** healthcare and **pension** systems. To **alleviate** these challenges, governments can expand **community** care services and promote **preventive** healthcare, while individuals maintain healthier lifestyles.

# D. Grammar for Band 7

## D1. Fix the collocation / article errors (10 min)

1. One reason of this problem is technology.  
2. This is a solution of ageing society.  
3. Technology effect people’s relationships.  
4. Government should to support local films.  
5. The life expectancy is increasing in many country.  
6. There have many advantages.  
7. It bring negative effects to society.  
8. People prefer foreign films because of the better quality.

### Answers — D1
1. One reason **for** this problem… / The reason **why**…  
2. a solution **to** **an** ageing society  
3. Technology **affects**… / has an **effect on**…  
4. Government**s** should support… *(delete to)*  
5. Life expectancy is increasing in many **countries**. *(usually no “the”)*  
6. There **are** many advantages.  
7. It **brings** negative effects…  
8. OK / …because **they have** better quality.

## D2. Combine into complex sentences (12 min)

1. People live longer. Pension costs rise. *(because)*  
2. Technology connects families abroad. Local meet-ups decline. *(while)*  
3. Foreign films have bigger budgets. They dominate cinemas. *(which)*  
4. Governments fund local films. Cultural identity is protected. *(so that)*  
5. Online chatting is convenient. It may lack emotional depth. *(although)*  
6. Community care expands. Hospital pressure falls. *(if)*

### Model — D2
1. Because people live longer, pension costs rise.  
2. While technology connects families abroad, local meet-ups may decline.  
3. Foreign films have bigger budgets, which helps them dominate cinemas.  
4. Governments fund local films so that cultural identity is protected.  
5. Although online chatting is convenient, it may lack emotional depth.  
6. If community care expands, hospital pressure falls.

## D3. Error paragraph (12 min)

> People living longer cause many problem for society. Firstly, government need pay more money for old people. Secondly, hospitals is busy. For the second question, people should healthy and government must solve it. In conclusion, ageing is bad but we can do something.

### Corrected version — D3
> People living longer **causes / Longer life expectancy causes** many **problems** for society. **One major issue is that governments** need **to** pay more money for **the elderly**. **In addition,** hospitals **are** busy. **As for solutions,** people should **stay** healthy and governments must **introduce practical measures such as community care**. **Overall,** an ageing population creates challenges, but they can be managed.

# E. Mixed Upgrades

## E1. Upgrade Q1 paragraph (ageing) (15 min)

**6.5**
> Firstly, old people make many problems. They need money and doctors. Also society is stressed. So ageing is bad for country.

**Tasks:** remove mechanical linker · add mechanism · upgrade 4 words · keep Q1 only

### Model Band 7 — E1
> One major problem caused by rising life expectancy is increased pressure on public services. Because older citizens often need long-term healthcare, hospitals and care homes face heavier demand. At the same time, pension systems become harder to sustain when a smaller workforce supports a larger retired population. As a result, both individuals and society carry a greater financial burden.

---

## E2. Upgrade Q2 paragraph (films / should) (15 min)

**6.5**
> Secondly, government should give money to local films. This is important for culture. If not, local films will die. So I agree.

**Tasks:** make stance conditional · add who/how/result · avoid extreme *die*

### Model Band 7 — E2
> In my view, governments should provide financial support to local film industries, but the support should be targeted. Grants and tax incentives can help local producers compete with high-budget foreign studios and protect cultural identity. However, funding should reward quality and audience engagement rather than protect weak productions automatically. In this way, local cinema can develop sustainably.

---

4. (Vocabulary) Replace “old people number up”: an __________ population / rising life __________

### Answers — E3

3. to
