IELTS Two-Part Question 6.5 → 7/7.5

IELTS Writing Teaching Plan — Two-Part Questions

题型： Task 2 Direct / Double Questions（两问型）

Student level: Band 6.5

Target: Band 7.0–7.5

1. 真题材料（Authentic-style prompts）

编号真题风格题目课堂角色
真题 ACambridge 高频In many countries, people are now living longer than ever before. Some people say an ageing population creates problems for governments. Other people think there are benefits for society. What are the advantages and disadvantages of an ageing population for society? (若课堂只用纯 two-part，改用下方标准双问)—
真题 A（主用）Cambridge IELTS 风格In many countries, people are living longer than ever before. What problems does this cause for individuals and society? What can individuals and governments do to deal with these problems?诊断 + 精修主线
真题 BCambridge IELTS 风格Nowadays the way many people interact with each other has changed because of technology. In what ways has technology affected the types of relationships people make? Has this become a positive or negative development?迁移：说明 + 评价
真题 C高频变体Many people prefer to watch foreign films rather than locally produced films. Why is this? Should governments give financial support to local film industries?限时模考：原因 + 观点

题型识别关键词

  • 两个直接问题（常为不同任务）：Why…? / What problems…? / What are the effects…?
  • 第二问常是：Do the advantages…? / Is this positive or negative? / What can be done…? / Should…?
  • 必须 两个问题都充分回答，不能偏废。

Two-part 常见组合

  1. Causes / Reasons + Solutions
  2. Problems / Effects + Solutions
  3. Reasons + Opinion (positive/negative or should…)
  4. Effects + Opinion
  5. Reasons + Measures / Recommendation

2. 为什么 6.5 学生在 Two-part 容易卡住

问题6.5 常见表现Band 7 目标
只答一问原因写很长，第二问草草两句两问篇幅大致均衡（约 50/50）
两问混在一段读起来乱，中心不清一问一段（或清晰的两段分工）
第二问跑题题目问 positive/negative，却只写解决方案严格按第二问任务写
例子空泛Technology is bad for relationships具体机制 + 例子
立场不清It has both sides 无决断评价类第二问要有清晰倾向

教学优先级

  1. 审题：拆出 Q1 / Q2 任务类型（TR）
  2. 均衡覆盖与段落分工（CC / TR）
  3. 因果 / 评价复杂句准确（GRA）
  4. 话题词汇自然升级（LR）

3. Band 7 推荐结构

结构 A（最稳，先教）

段落任务
Introduction改写话题 + 用一句话预告将回答两个问题
Body 1完整回答 Question 1
Body 2完整回答 Question 2
Conclusion各用一句收束两个答案（不引入新点）

结构 B（第二问是 opinion 时）

  • BP1：Reasons / Effects（Q1）
  • BP2：Clear opinion + support（Q2）
  • 结论：重申评价立场

时间

  • 计划 5 分钟（必须写出 Q1/Q2 关键词）
  • 写作 32–33 分钟
  • 检查 2–3 分钟（专查：两问是否都答了）

5. 详细教案

Lesson 1 — 诊断与目标设定（90 min）

真题 A

In many countries, people are living longer than ever before. What problems does this cause for individuals and society? What can individuals and governments do to deal with these problems?

流程

  1. 热身（10 min）

问：这篇要回答几个问题？两问一样重要吗？

  1. 限时写作（40 min）
  2. 自查（15 min）
  • Q1 有独立充分段落
  • Q2 有独立充分段落
  • 结论覆盖两问
  • 无跑题
  1. 口头反馈（25 min） — Top 3 only

作业

  • 只重写 introduction（明确预告两问）。
  • 建 Error Log。

Lesson 2 — 架构与审题拆分（90 min）

目标： 装上 “Q1 一段 + Q2 一段” 系统。

板书拆题公式

Step 1: 话题是什么？

Step 2: Question 1 任务类型？

Step 3: Question 2 任务类型？

Step 4: 每问准备 2 个可展开点

真题 A 示范计划

问题任务内容点
Q1Problems for individuals & society个人：医疗开支/生活质量；社会：养老金与医疗系统压力
Q2What individuals & governments can do个人：健康生活方式；政府：养老金改革、社区养老与医疗投入

流程

  1. 拆题训练（20 min）— 3 道 two-part 只拆不写
  2. 结构建模（20 min）
  3. “偏科文”对比（15 min）：Q2 只有两句的 6.5 范文 vs 均衡 7.0
  4. 写 Body 1（Q1）限时（25 min）
  5. 收束（10 min）

课堂练习： A1 题型识别 · A2 均衡检查 · A3 连接词 · A4 写 Q1 段

作业

  • 真题 A 全文（强调 Q2 不少于 Q1 的 80% 字数）。
  • A5 第二问任务匹配。

Lesson 3 — Q1 深挖（90 min）

目标： 把 “problems / reasons / effects” 写透，不列点。

发展链

Point → Why / How → Example → Result for individuals or society

升级示例

  • 6.5：Old people need more money and hospitals are busy.
  • 7.0：Because longer lifespans increase demand for long-term healthcare, public hospitals face heavier caseloads; at the same time, pension systems struggle when fewer workers support more retirees.

流程

  1. 作业反馈（20 min）— 先看两问是否都出现
  2. Q1 机制写作坊（30 min）
  3. 限时重写 BP1（25 min）
  4. 选点训练：删掉最弱的第三点（15 min）

练习： B1 搭配 · B2 词汇升级 · B3 词形

作业

  • 定稿 Q1 段 + 自查表。
  • B4 短语造句。

Lesson 4 — Q2 深挖（solutions / opinion）（90 min）

目标： 第二问写满，且任务正确。

若 Q2 = solutions

  • Who + How + Expected result
  • 尽量对应 Q1 的问题（matching）

若 Q2 = positive/negative 或 should…

  • 明确立场（总体积极 / 总体消极 / 有条件支持）
  • 2 个支持理由 + 1 个具体例子
  • 可用一句让步，但不能变成无立场

真题 A 对应表

Q1 problemQ2 response
Healthcare pressureGovernments expand community care / preventive health
Pension burdenGradual retirement age reform + sustainable funding
Individual loneliness / costsIndividuals plan savings; stay active; family + community support

流程

  1. 空泛方案清理（15 min）
  2. 对应表填空（20 min）
  3. 限时写 BP2（30 min）
  4. 结论两句操练（20 min）
  5. 总结（5 min）

练习： B5 完型 · D1 搭配 · D2 复合句

作业

  • 真题 A 二稿（两问字数差控制在 20% 内）。
  • D3 改错。

Lesson 5 — Lexis & Grammar Lift（90 min）

高频雷区

  • 漏答第二问标记词（should / positive / problems）
  • a solution of → a solution to
  • the reason of → the reason for / why
  • affect / effect 混用
  • although… but

高价值句型

  1. One major problem is that…
  2. This can be addressed by…
  3. In terms of relationships, technology has…
  4. Overall, I would argue that this is largely a positive/negative development because…

流程

  1. Error Log（20 min）
  2. 双问衔接句（20 min）
  3. 话题词库：老龄化 / 科技社交（25 min）
  4. 最弱段重写（25 min）

练习： D4–D6 · E1 段落升级

作业

  • 句型转换 10 题。
  • 词库各 8 句。

Lesson 6 — 迁移真题 B（90 min）

真题 B

Nowadays the way many people interact with each other has changed because of technology. In what ways has technology affected the types of relationships people make? Has this become a positive or negative development?

审题重点

  • Q1：in what ways = 影响/变化方式（不是简单利弊清单）
  • Q2：positive or negative = 必须表态

推荐计划

  • BP1：线上弱连接变多；跨距离关系更易维持；部分面对面深度下降
  • BP2：总体 mixed but largely positive / largely negative（选一侧并支持）

流程

  1. 5 分钟拆题比赛（10 min）
  2. 计划板（20 min）
  3. 限时双主体段（40 min）
  4. 与真题 A 迁移复盘（20 min）

练习： C1–C4 科技/人际关系词汇

作业

  • 真题 B 全文。
  • 用荧光笔标出 Q1 句与 Q2 句。

Lesson 7 — 限时真题 C（90 min）

真题 C

Many people prefer to watch foreign films rather than locally produced films. Why is this? Should governments give financial support to local film industries?

计划示范

  • Q1 Why：制作预算与特效；全球明星与营销；英语片国际传播
  • Q2 Should：有条件支持 — 保护文化多样性，但应透明、鼓励质量而非保护落后

流程

  1. 策略复述（10 min）
  2. 全程限时（40 min）
  3. 与 Lesson 1 对比（25 min）
  4. E3 综合小测（15 min）

作业

  • 重写更弱的一问。
  • 继续 Section F。

Lesson 8 — 终测与反馈（90 min）

  1. 新 two-part 模考（40–45 min）
  2. 四项评分会议（35 min）
  3. 考前维护（10 min）：每周 3 篇 two-part；每天检查 “两问是否均衡”

7. Band 7 自查清单

  • 两个问题都回答了
  • BP1 = Q1，BP2 = Q2（不混写）
  • 两段篇幅大致均衡
  • 第二问任务类型正确（solution ≠ opinion）
  • 评价类第二问有明确立场
  • 结论覆盖两问，无新论点
  • 使用 2–3 个准确复杂句
  • 5+ 话题搭配

8. 教师示范方向

真题 A（长寿）

  • Q1：healthcare & pension pressure; possible loneliness/care burden for families
  • Q2：healthy ageing habits; government investment in community care & sustainable pensions
  • Lexis：life expectancy, ageing population, pension system, long-term care, preventive healthcare

真题 B（科技与关系）

  • Q1：more online/long-distance ties; broader weak ties; sometimes less face-to-face depth
  • Q2：clear overall judgement (e.g. largely positive if used balanced)
  • Lexis：interact, maintain relationships, face-to-face contact, social media, sense of community

真题 C（电影）

  • Q1：budgets, effects, global marketing, language reach
  • Q2：conditional yes — cultural identity + quality incentives
  • Lexis：locally produced films, box-office appeal, cultural identity, subsidies, film industry

A. Task Analysis, Balance & Cohesion

A1. Identify Q1 / Q2 types (8 min)

For each prompt, label Q1 and Q2:

reasons · problems · effects/ways · solutions · opinion (positive/negative) · should

  1. People live longer. What problems does this cause? What can be done?
  2. Technology has changed relationships. In what ways? Is this positive or negative?
  3. Many prefer foreign films. Why? Should governments support local films?
  4. More people work from home. Why? What effects does this have on society?
  5. Children use smartphones early. Why is this happening? Do the advantages outweigh the disadvantages?

Answers — A1

  1. Q1 problems · Q2 solutions
  2. Q1 effects/ways · Q2 opinion
  3. Q1 reasons · Q2 should
  4. Q1 reasons · Q2 effects
  5. Q1 reasons · Q2 opinion (outweigh)

A2. Balance check (8 min)

Which essay plan is better for Band 7? Why?

Plan X

  • Intro
  • Long paragraph on problems only
  • Two sentences on solutions
  • Conclusion

Plan Y

  • Intro
  • Full paragraph on problems
  • Full paragraph on solutions
  • Conclusion covering both

Answer — A2

Plan Y — both questions are developed; Plan X under-answers Q2 and risks a TR ceiling at 6.5.

A3. Choose the best linker (10 min)

  1. Life expectancy is rising. (As a result / Although / For instance), pension systems face greater pressure.
  2. Technology helps friends stay in touch across countries. (However / Therefore / Due to), some local face-to-face contact may decline.
  3. Foreign films often have bigger budgets. (Because / For example / In order to), Hollywood productions attract global audiences with advanced effects.
  4. Governments can support local cinema. (This can be done by / Because of / Whereas) offering grants to high-quality local projects.
  5. Remote friendships are easier to maintain. (Overall / For instance / Despite), I would argue this is largely a positive development.

Answers — A3

  1. As a result
  2. However
  3. For example
  4. This can be done by
  5. Overall

A4. Write a Q1-only paragraph (15 min)

Prompt: What problems does an ageing population cause for society?

Write 90–120 words. Use at least 3 linkers. One central idea cluster only.

Teacher check

  • Answers Q1 only
  • Mechanism explained
  • Example present
  • No solutions mixed in

B5. Gap-fill — ageing two-part paragraph (10 min)

Rising life __________ means many countries now have an __________ population. One major problem is increased __________ on __________ healthcare and __________ systems. D. Grammar for Band 7

D1. Fix the collocation / article errors (10 min)

  1. One reason of this problem is technology.
  2. This is a solution of ageing society.
  3. Technology effect people’s relationships.
  4. Government should to support local films.
  5. The life expectancy is increasing in many country.
  6. There have many advantages.
  7. It bring negative effects to society.
  8. People prefer foreign films because of the better quality.

Answers — D1

  1. One reason for this problem… / The reason why…
  2. a solution to an ageing society
  3. Technology affects… / has an effect on…
  4. Governments should support… (delete to)
  5. Life expectancy is increasing in many countries. (usually no “the”)
  6. There are many advantages.

Model Band 7 — E1

One major problem caused by rising life expectancy is increased pressure on public services. Because older citizens often need long-term healthcare, hospitals and care homes face heavier demand. At the same time, pension systems become harder to sustain when a smaller workforce supports a larger retired population. As a result, both individuals and society carry a greater financial burden.
