## 二、Q27–30 选择题精讲（答案句标注）

### Q27｜第二段作者在做什么？ → **A**

**选项：**  
A pinpointing some **key changes** in our understanding of prehistory  
B outlining aspects still **poorly understood**  
C summarising **attitudes** towards revelations  
D overview of **disagreements** among archaeologists  

**同义替换：** pinpointing key changes ≈ draw out overall messages / Two things stand out  

**✅ 原文答案句：**
> **However, I do think it’s possible to draw out some overall messages from the blizzard of archaeological finds in recent years. Two things stand out to me. One is … advanced behaviours … much further back … And the other is that we have badly misunderstood gender roles …**

**排除：** B 不是“仍不懂”；C 不是别人态度；D 第二段无考古学家分歧。

---

### Q28｜第六段提手机是为了说明？ → **A**

**选项：**  
A most developments happen in a **gradual** way  
B innovation from a **variety of sources**  
C not all advancements are **positive**  
D evolution path often **unpredictable**  

**同义替换：** gradual ≈ incremental steps  

**✅ 原文答案句：**
> **Evolution usually works by incremental steps and so does technology. The first birds weren’t great at flying, and the first mobile phones weren’t great at, well, anything really.**

**重点支撑句：**
> But there was never that much evidence that human evolution worked this way（突然爆发）. Instead, it seems the Neanderthals and many others all walked so we could run.

**排除：** 手机例子服务“渐进”，不是来源多样/好坏/不可预测。

---

### Q29｜‘unpick this stuff’ 指什么？ → **D**

**选项：**  
A assessing impact of **recent** findings  
B questioning **authenticity** of earlier evidence  
C conducting research into how societies were **organised**  
D **reevaluating research influenced by outdated beliefs** about society  

**同义替换：** unpick this stuff ≈ 拆解旧父权观念对研究的渗透；outdated beliefs ≈ unfashionably patriarchal views  

**✅ 原文答案句：**
> **Archaeology was invented by individuals with now unfashionably patriarchal views about gender, and those notions fed into their research. Today’s researchers are trying to unpick this stuff…**

**排除：** 不是质疑证据真伪（B），而是质疑**观念污染研究**。

---

### Q30｜末段作者暗示什么？ → **A**

**选项：**  
A Studying past societies could help us create a **fairer society today**  
B should not **judge** past by modern standards  
C still **much to learn** about evolution of societies  
D **more than one way** to interpret evidence  

**同义替换：** fairer / choose differently ≈ Inequality… aren’t inevitable. They’re choices  

**✅ 原文答案句：**
> **Inequality, authoritarianism and patriarchy aren’t inevitable. They’re choices, and prehistory shows us that we can choose differently.**

**重点句（承接）：**
> It seems that the more we find out about past societies, the more our preconceptions about the ways society ‘has to be’ turn out to be wrong.

## 三、Q31–34 句尾匹配精讲

| 题 | 题干 | 答案 | 同义替换 | ✅ 原文答案句 |
|----|------|------|----------|----------------|
| **31** | Kalambo Falls revealed that | **E** | sophisticated workmanship ≈ shaped… interlocked… structure / building；not only Homo sapiens ≈ 476,000 years… before Homo sapiens | **At Kalambo Falls… buried logs… shaped with stone tools so that they interlocked… part of a larger structure… 476,000 years old… almost 200,000 years before our species, Homo sapiens, evolved.** |
| **32** | high-altitude regions suggests that | **F** | harsh environments ≈ frozen heights / high-altitude；before Homo sapiens ≈ extinct hominins… 200,000 years ago | **…extinct hominins such as the Denisovans lived on the frozen heights of high-altitude regions 200,000 years ago – upending the old notion that such environments were only settled by modern humans around 3,600 years ago.** |
| **33** | June 2023 shows that | **D** | who looked for food ≈ hunted；mistaken ≈ demolition of ‘Man the Hunter’ | **…a meta-analysis published in June 2023… found women hunted in 80 per cent of them.** |
| **34** | Iberian leader indicates that | **B** | who had power ≈ leader；inaccurate assumptions ≈ female, not male as many had assumed | **…an Iberian leader from around 4000 years ago turned out to be female, not male as many had assumed…** |

**干扰项速记：**  
- A（智人更早出现）→ 在 §1，与 31–34 四题干无关  
- C（灭绝人属性别角色不同）→ 文中未直接比较 extinct vs sapiens 性别角色

## 四、Q35–40 YES / NO / NOT GIVEN 精讲

| 题 | 陈述 | 答案 | 判定逻辑 | ✅ 原文答案句 / 重点句 |
|----|------|------|----------|------------------------|
| **35** | 尼安德特洞穴画**很可能是史上最早**艺术品 | **NO** | 作者认为更早物种也可能做艺术，否定“尼安德特最早” | **Art also seems to have been invented by older hominins.** … Neanderthals painted… **Even earlier species, such as Homo erectus, may also have made art…** |
| **36** | 贝壳上史前雕刻**非常罕见** | **NG** | 只举例 shells，未评 rare/common | 仅有：*engraving patterns on shells*（无 rare） |
| **37** | 考察 Rising Star 的**方法**很不传统 | **NG** | 不传统的是**发表方式**，非考察方法 | **…heightened by the way the results were released, in a non-traditional journal…**（谈 publicise，不谈 examine methods） |
| **38** | Rising Star 蚀刻**年代不清楚** | **YES** | 明确未牢固定年 | **…etchings… though these have yet to be firmly dated.** ＋ **more evidence is needed… with regard to the dating of the etchings.** |
| **39** | **宣传方式**加剧了争议 | **YES** | dispute heightened by release method | **The dispute has only been heightened by the way the results were released, in a non-traditional journal…** |
| **40** | 脑**大小**是能否产艺术的**关键因素** | **NO** | 作者认为脑容量是 distraction，非关键 | **…I think the species’ small brains are a distraction.** … **other properties, such as the brain’s internal wiring… may explain… complex behaviours, despite their small brains.** |

**Y/N/NG 口诀：** YES=同向改写｜NO=作者明确唱反调｜NG=原文没说题干这个点（常偷换 examine↔release、加 rare 等）

## 七、课堂流程建议

1. **10′** 扫 §2 两大主旨 → 学生用中文复述  
2. **25′** 精讲答案句：27–30（段内定位 + 排除）  
3. **15′** 31–34 专名扫描（Kalambo / high-altitude / June 2023 / Iberian）  
4. **20′** 35–40：先判“作者态度词”，再贴答案句  
5. **10′** 搭配朗读 + 造句  
6. **10′** 错题：尤其 37（方法 vs 发表）、40（distraction≠key factor）
